About Famous Barber

Started from the bottom and now we're here, literally! Indulge in our story below.

Our story, mission and vision

Famous Barber opened in 2024 as a single-chair barbershop on Esselen Street, Sunnyside. Our founder Onthathile Phalatse had one rule: never rush a cut. Word spread through the neighbourhood, and by 2025 we had opened a second branch in Hatfield. Every visit still ends the same way it did back then, with a proper hot towel finish, no matter how busy the shop is.

To deliver precise, unrushed haircuts and an old-school barbershop experience to every client who sits in our chair.

To become the most trusted name in men's grooming across Gauteng.

Our barbers

Every chair at Famous Barber is run by a barber with years of experience behind the clippers, trained on fades, tapers and classic scissor cuts. We take our time on every detail, because precision is the whole point.
